Spectral Twins (DAEP)

Explore the latent space of a diffusion autoencoder (DAEP) trained on supernova spectra. Select a spectrum to identify its spectroscopic twins through latent space nearest-neighbor searches using cosine similarity, compare their spectra, and examine the model's reconstructions.
Model and data: Henna Abunemeh (UIUC).
